# `tamarin/compliant/`

## 1. What this model demonstrates

- A participant that runs the nomination check signs no record attributing to it a witness it did not nominate (`no_signature_over_a_misattributed_nominee`), **per attributed witness**: the record carries two witnesses attributed to the signer and the conforming signer checks both. A signer that checks the first and authorises the body falsifies it, which the gate runs.
- A participant that runs the directional check nominates no witness that was in its own neighbourhood **at the time** (`no_nominee_from_the_nominators_own_neighbourhood`); a witness that joins that neighbourhood later does not unmake the nomination. The check is the semantics of the conforming rule and nothing deeper, so its twin is stated: a participant that skips it nominates an own-neighbour (`an_own_neighbour_is_nominated_without_the_check`).
- Among participants who keep the signing discipline, an accepted record implies the meeting happened (`a_record_between_conforming_participants_implies_a_meeting`), and one meeting's signatures bind one roster (`a_conforming_signature_binds_one_roster`). The carve-out names the participants the record names, so the claim holds for them beside any number of parties elsewhere who did not keep it.
- Among verifiers who keep it, an accepted recovery implies a meeting (`recovery_between_conforming_parties_requires_a_meeting`), and a recognition names the key that meeting exchanged (`a_conforming_recognition_names_the_meeting_key`). The carve-out names the verifier the patron relied on, not every verifier in the trace.
- Sealing a line before taking a reissue leaves nothing for a holder of the old key to supersede (`sealing_first_leaves_nothing_to_supersede`); reissuing first leaves a window, and the window is a **race**: the witness is reissue, then the thief supersedes, then the seal arrives too late (`reissuing_first_leaves_a_window`), not reissue-and-never-seal.
- A session opened in failover performs no trust-bearing operation (`no_trust_bearing_operation_on_a_sibling`), **per session**: a node holds one record of a binding and as many sessions under it as the client opens, each with its own role read at attach time, and a session ending and a fresh one opening under the same binding is an ordinary reachable trace (`reconnection_is_reachable`).
- Once a node holds supersession evidence for a binding, no attach under it follows (`no_attach_after_supersession`, under the bound): the record is what the node remembers, and an attach needs it. A node that ends every session and keeps the record, then serves a reconnect, falsifies it, which the gate runs. An earlier version forbade any second attach in a binding's whole history, which made this true by fiat and the ordinary reconnect unrepresentable.
- A server that defers TLS 1.3 0-RTT early data binds session state only after the handshake **of the connection that carried the early data** has completed (`a_conforming_server_binds_only_after_the_handshake`); a completed handshake on an earlier connection with the same client admits nothing. Letting any completed handshake serve falsifies it, which the gate runs.
- Queued material is delivered only under the credential it was queued for (`delivery_is_to_the_credential_it_was_queued_for`): the queue carries the recipient keyhash, as design §14.1.6 has it, so an item queued under a credential later superseded does not go out under the successor. Ignoring the queued credential at delivery falsifies it, which the gate runs; the supersession half is in the bounded companion.
- A key enters an issuer's record once, by one of two doors (`a_key_enters_a_record_once`, `a_record_key_entered_by_one_of_two_doors`).
- The non-compliant traces remain reachable, and each witness is one the wire would accept as well formed: collusion between **two distinct** participants produces a record with no meeting; a lying verifier, **distinct from the subject**, completes a recovery; misattribution succeeds when the check is skipped; an own-neighbour is nominated when that check is skipped; a thief who signs in a line **after** the subject sealed it is still taken by a counterparty holding no chain, the thief's own record and not some record; a sibling that has not received a rotation still issues for the old key; a server that acts on early data binds a **replayed** `Attach` on the connection the replay opened, one whose handshake never completes, and binds the same `Attach` on both connections.

## 2. What it cannot demonstrate

- That any node keeps these commitments. Every property is conditional on conformance, and no third party can check conformance.
- Anything cryptographic. Signatures are abstracted away.
- That issuance never follows supersession, that nothing is served under a superseded credential, or that no attach follows supersession. The first two are checked in `tla/SupersessionDiscipline.tla`; here all three hold only under a bound of one supersession, two services and two attaches, since the binding record is consumed and restored by every attach and service and the unbounded search regresses through them. The bound lives in a `.bounded` fragment that `run-all.sh` splices into the theory before its final `end`, so the rules have one source; the fragment is not a theory on its own.
- That physical co-presence occurred, or that a recognition is truthful.
- That a rotation reaches any particular issuer. Records are per-node and replication lag is modelled, not resolved.
- That a successor key is fresh. `currency` mints it fresh because design §9.1 binds the old identity *"to a new keypair"*, and carries the subject presenting a key it held before as a labelled assumption (`TheSuccessorIsANewKeypair`), not a result; whether a counterparty could check it is not established.
- Whether a node must refuse to **enqueue** for a binding it knows superseded. The requirement is that nothing further is delivered, queue included; enqueueing afterwards is permitted here and useless, and no claim about refusing it is made.
- Witness sets larger than two, in either direction: two is enough to represent checking one attribution and signing the body, and not enough for a rule whose failure needs three.
- That the reference client is the only conforming one. These are obligations, not an implementation.
