## Executive Verdict

FuerosNet is an unusually disciplined, intellectually serious protocol-and-reference-implementation effort. Its strongest engineering qualities are rare even in mature open-source systems: an iterative, fuzz-tested deterministic CBOR decoder with an iterative `Drop` destructor; zero Clippy warnings (`-D warnings`) across 21 crates; a single production `unsafe` call in 107k LOC of Rust; 21 specification-to-code/vector SHA-256 pins; 561 of 567 active acceptance entries implemented; and an unusually candid assumptions/risk register ([network-design.md](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L8874-L9008)).

At the same time, rapid iteration (10–38 commits/day throughout November 2026, including major late-November pivots from Signal/PQXDH to a bespoke split seal, from Wasmtime to OCI container hosting, and from WebView to GeckoView) has opened **four critical security vulnerabilities in shipped code**, **dozens of semantic contradictions across authoritative documents**, **significant formal-model staleness (148 spec commits behind)**, and **sharp tensions between the operational complexity of the stack and its stated goal of serving ordinary local communities**.

## 2. Code Quality & Security Audit

### 2.1 Genuine Engineering Strengths
- **Defensive wire parsing (`rhtn-codec`):** Strictly iterative CBOR parser (`Vec<Frame>`), checked arithmetic (`try_from`/`checked_add`), no pre-allocation from untrusted counts, zero `unsafe`/`panic!`, and an **iterative `Drop` for `Item`** ([cbor.rs](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/crates/codec/src/cbor.rs#L46-L72)) added after libFuzzer triggered stack overflow on 56 KB of nested arrays.

## 3. General Suitability to Purpose

Evaluated against [network-design.md §1](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L104-L249): *anchoring trust in physical human presence, with no global state or central honeypot, to serve ordinary communities (families, bowling leagues, churches, small businesses) at physical-world affiliation privacy.*

### 3.1 Where the Design Genuinely Succeeds
- **Coherent anti-centralisation principle ("visibility replaces enforcement", §1.1):** Refusing unenforceable `MUST`s and pushing distinctions into the non-pluggable evidence schema is architecturally sound and consistently applied across the wire protocol.
- **Subject-keyed sealed capture store (§7.5.2):** Turning biometric retention into a cryptographic key-release discipline is a standout privacy design — a seized compliant phone yields only `XChaCha20-Poly1305` ciphertext of counterparties' faces.
- **Mathematically grounded cross-horizon trust metric (§16.2):** Replacing naive distance decay with setwise-conserving Advogato-style max-flow over a collapsed-horizon graph (verified in `models/simulation/flow_metric.py`) properly bounds Sybil regions behind an honest cut.
- **Honest threat-model epistemology (§17–§20):** Explicitly listing 30 unsourced claims, 32 load-bearing unvalidated assumptions, and 28 chosen operating points avoids the security-theatre common in decentralised protocol papers.

### 3.2 Structural Suitability Gaps & Tensions

1. **The Operational Complexity vs. Target-Audience Mismatch (Largest Suitability Risk):**
   The §1 thesis targets families, clubs, churches, and small businesses. Yet operating a subnet's node currently requires: renting a static-IPv4 cloud VPS; minting cloud-provider API tokens; renewing a **90-day delegated credential run** (`45 × 48h`) from the root phone before the subnet loses its node; vetting OCI container manifests and installing images by SHA-256 digest; managing role predicates/grants; manually reattaching the root phone to **reopen sealed packages after every unattended OS security reboot** ([infra-client-requirements.md](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/infra-client-requirements.md#L1246-L1249)); and troubleshooting via SSH. Meanwhile, the mobile client is **Android arm64-only** (357 MB debug APK due to bundled GeckoView, [burn-down.md](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/Robot/burn-down.md#L151)), with **zero iOS implementation** (`crates/mobile/ios/` is a 15-line README) — excluding ~50%+ of ordinary Western community members on day one. In practice, this operational burden will strongly push non-technical communities onto managed hosting services, reproducing the exact cloud concentration that [§18.1](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L7979-L7985) names as the #1 systemic risk.

2. **The 4-Minute In-Person Ceremony Ergonomics & Fragility (`A1`, `A9`, `A11`, `A16`):**
   Requiring ordinary people to maintain continuous camera-on-face contact for **≥4 minutes** (`≥90%` presence, max 20 s gap, randomised head-turn prompts, live remote witness observation over mobile data) is a severe social UX hurdle. Live-witness availability in communities of sleeping mobile phones naturally biases witness sets toward always-on infra nodes (which [§18.4](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L8303-L8308) notes an adversary can game via uptime). Meanwhile, `YuNet + SFace int8` uses an **unmeasured** provisional fuzzing parameter (`FUZZ_SIGMA = 0.03`, [engine/src/lib.rs](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/crates/engine/src/lib.rs#L79)), randomised motion prompts are unvalidated against 2026 real-time deepfake/video-injection attacks (`A16`), and high UX friction incentivises users to fall back on former-patron transfer countersignatures (`A34`), thinning actual face-to-face grounding.

3. **The Max-Flow Metric Does Not Govern the Primary Use Case:**
   [§16.2](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L7368-L7376) and [§17.3](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L7964-L7973) exempt the 2-edge trust horizon (up to 221 identities) from max-flow rationing. Yet `A2` assumes most traffic stays inside the 2-edge horizon, and [resource-requirements.md §7.1.1](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/resource-requirements.md) scopes resource access to horizon members. Consequently, **the signature Sybil-resistance math is bypassed for everyday subnet communication and resource access**, leaving horizon security dependent on patron vetting and `A34` transfers.

4. **Split-Seal Forward Secrecy vs. 30-Day Delivered Retention:**
   Replacing Signal's Double Ratchet with the bespoke split seal ([§14.2.4](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L6562-L6707)) eliminates per-message forward secrecy and post-compromise security (PCS now requires full identity rotation). More subtly, because `delivered-retention` defaults to **30 days** for multi-device catch-up ([daemon/src/config.rs](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/crates/daemon/src/config.rs#L108)) and `NodeKeyService::prune` retains every private X-Wing key referenced by any message still in `DirStore` ([node_keys.rs](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/crates/node/src/node_keys.rs#L323-L327), [runtime.rs](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/crates/node/src/runtime.rs#L922)), the serving node holds **up to 30 days of daily private X-Wing keys on disk simultaneously**. A stolen recipient key/device can immediately pull 30 days of delivered messages + `ssN` from an honest node, and a compromised node snapshot + recipient key decrypts 30 days of recorded ciphertext — substantially widening the "1 day" intuition of [§14.2.4.3](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L6619-L6624). Furthermore, as [§14.2.4.6](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L6700-L6705) itself warns, the split-seal composition has had **no formal cryptographic analysis**.

5. **Public Card Spam/DoS & Unconfined `live` Media Egress:**
   - Because minted submission keys hide the sender from the recipient's serving node ([§14.2.2](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/network-design.md#L6541-L6545)), anyone who obtains a user's public Card can fill their per-subordinate mailbox cap (256 MB) anonymously, blocking legitimate mail until collected. This undermines publishing Cards on websites, email signatures, or business cards.
   - Because `live` WebRTC sessions bypass the local proxy and the client cannot enforce `media_endpoints` ([resource-requirements.md §3](file:///home/aspiringsavage/Documents/FuerosNet/resource-requirements.md#L187-L198)), approving one `live` session temporarily voids the entire GeckoView/WebKitGTK frame-isolation boundary that was adopted at a +170 MB APK cost.

6. **Mobile CGNAT & Relay Economics (`A15`):**
   Two mobile phones behind carrier-grade NAT is the *normal* deployment environment for a phone-first social network, not an edge case. If direct traversal fails frequently, relay load shifts back onto single-VPS patron nodes (alongside media ports, boards, mailboxes, and OCI containers), stressing the ~\$20/month single-VM economic premise (`A15`, `A19`).
